## Accounts and Memberships

- Account owns globally unique lowercase email, password and Sessions. Member owns name and Role, once per Account per Organization. Account `/me` and the web switcher expose active Organizations. Organization `/me` resolves only its active Member. Every Organization route uses the authenticated Account and URL Organization. Unknown Organization and absent/inactive Membership share 404 `organization_unavailable`.
- Email/password sign-in issues a web HttpOnly cookie with identity/expiry only in the body, or a native bearer without cookies. Both share throttling and sign-out. Valid sign-out is 204 and revokes only that Session. On `DELETE /api/v1/session` only, missing/invalid/expired/revoked credentials give 401, expire the configured and legacy cookies (one name on local HTTP) and make no writes; other routes give 401 without `Set-Cookie`. Missing/disallowed cookie WebSocket Origin gives 403 `origin_not_allowed` before Auth and Organization resolution.
- Email, setup Organization/Member names and Channel names reject NUL with 400 `ErrorBody` before DB/password work but after Auth/setup-token gates. Both sign-ins validate email before throttle. Passwords and Invite tokens follow their own rules.

## Password and Account Session security (#304)

- Wrong current password gives 403 `current_password_incorrect` and keeps Settings and Session usable. 401 means a missing/invalid/expired/revoked Session, including loss while waiting for the Account lock. Invalid new length gives 400 `invalid_new_password`. Malformed input or empty/oversized current password gives 400 `invalid input`. Setup and password change share Argon2 and length rules.
- Shared sign-in IP/email budgets deny 429 `too many sign-in attempts` with `Retry-After` before Argon2 admission (503 `password admission unavailable`). Only incorrect passwords count. Refusals preserve all passwords and Sessions.
- Password change and revoke-others keep the current Session and sockets. Atomic cross-Organization revocation includes earlier serialized cookie/bearer sign-ins. HTTP cancellation cannot interrupt commit and 4001 signals ([S4](realtime.md#real-time-sync-contract)). Settings prompts for the current password and confirms other-Session logout. Owner-led recovery is out of scope.

## Members, Roles and Invites

| Role | Invite and manage Invites | Change Roles | Deactivate others | Create Channels | List active Members |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Any Role | Yes | Any Role | Yes | All |
| Admin | Admin, Member, Guest | No | Admin, Member, Guest | Yes | All |
| Member | No | No | No | Yes | All |
| Guest | No | No | No | No | Self and shared |

- Deny by default. Directory lists only id/name/Role in name/id order. Owner/Admin/Member list every active Member; a Guest lists exactly itself plus active current-revision Members with a usable grant on a Channel the Guest can access (public access alone does not share). Directory, caller Role/revision and grants share a fresh non-locking snapshot. Web refreshes on Organization selection, reconnect, Channel loss/list change and every member-panel open. Stale responses cannot overwrite newer loads. [Notice visibility and invalidation](realtime.md#organization-activity-notices-307-implemented)
- Sender names use directory → readable-Message author lookup → メンバー. Lookup includes departed/deactivated authors without widening the directory. Omit unknown, foreign and unreadable authors. Deduplicate results, allow empty input and deny >100 input ids before deduplication (400 `invalid input`). Authorization, authorship and identity share a non-locking read-only snapshot. Prior revocation denies. Directory reload resets lookup results. Omissions/failures await reload, Channel selection or reconnect, without polling/push.
- Every Role needs a usable private grant, and Guests also need one for public Channels. History, writes, subscribe, replay and fan-out read fresh Role, activity, revision and grants. DMs require both pair participation and a usable grant. DM grant APIs deny even GET, no-ops and self-leave after access checks. All four DM Channel Capabilities deny `dm_membership_fixed`. DMs have no manager rights or last-grant/manager guards. [DM open and renewal](dms.md#open-and-renewal-b2)
- Organization and Channel Capabilities mirror `authorize` as UI hints. Every request reauthorizes.
- Invites bind normalized email/Role, expire and are single-use. Disclose the token once, store only its hash, use POST bodies and fragment-only links, strip incoming fragments from history immediately and keep credentials out of web storage. Email verification/sending are out of scope.
- Acceptance requires an authenticated matching Account for existing emails (anonymous 409 `invite_sign_in_required`, mismatch 403 `invite_account_mismatch`). Anonymous signup is only for unused emails, even in a race. Invalid supplied Account Sessions give 401 without anonymous fallback. Invalid/used/revoked/expired/unknown/foreign Invite tokens or wrong supplied email share 404 `invite_unavailable`. Explicit stale-cookie recovery retains the Invite and requires manual resubmit after sign-out 204/401. [Web fencing and recovery](../decisions/web.md)
- Check expiry at atomic consumption/activation. Existing active Members get 409 `member_already_active` with no Role change. Reactivation reuses id/revision with invited Role/name and no revived grants/rights. Acceptance rechecks active issuer authority for that Role or gives 403 `invite_issuer_unauthorized`.
- List at most 100 outstanding Invites per page, in creation/id order without tokens. Pre-fill filtering hides Owner-Role counts from Admins. Keysets survive consumption/expiry/revocation. No continuation on the last page. Malformed/unknown/foreign Organization or Role cursors give 400 `invalid input` without details. Manage other issuers' Invites even after issuer authority loss. Member/Guest management gives 403 `forbidden`. Unavailable Organizations retain their 404. Revocation of accepted/expired/revoked/unknown/foreign/unmanageable Invites shares 404 `invite_unavailable`. Revoke-first denies acceptance without changes. Accept-first denies revoke. Issuer promotion/reactivation cannot revive revocation.
- The Member panel pages outstanding Invites and confirms revocation in Japanese.
- <a name="member-endpoint-error-precedence"></a>With a valid Organization id, Role changes and deactivation first resolve Auth: 401 for a missing or malformed credential, then 400 `invalid input` if any path segment fails to percent-decode (including the Member `{id}`), then 401 for an invalid Account Session, then 404 `organization_unavailable` for unavailable caller Membership. Role changes then extract the JSON body (400 invalid input, 413 oversized, 415 unsupported media). Both return 400 `invalid input` for a decoded Member `{id}` that is not a UUID before the transaction's caller recheck and base authorization.
- Role changes serialize per Organization and recheck the actor under a Member lock held through COMMIT. The error order after input validation is 404 `organization_unavailable` for caller Membership or revision loss → 403 `forbidden` unless the caller is an Owner → 409 `last_owner` → 404 `member_not_found` for an unknown, foreign or inactive target → 409 `last_channel_manager`. Concurrent requests and direct SQL cannot demote or deactivate the last active Owner. The manager guard permits Owner-to-Admin and already-unmanaged Channels. Organization removal is out of scope.

### Member deactivation (#305, #237)

- Member id only. After [Auth and input validation](#member-endpoint-error-precedence), the error order is 404 `organization_unavailable` for caller Membership or revision loss → 403 `forbidden` unless the caller is an Owner or Admin → 404 `member_not_found` for an unknown, foreign or inactive target (including repeat) → 403 `forbidden` for self or Admin targeting Owner → 409 `last_owner` → 409 `last_channel_manager`. The matrix makes `last_owner` unreachable for a valid caller because sole-Owner self-deactivation is denied first. Keep the defensive check and database guard. Denials make no writes and preserve Memberships, revisions, grants, rights, Unread, Sessions and sockets. Permitted UI actions confirm in Japanese.
- Keep identity and authorship, bump revision and stale old grants/rights. Only affected Organization sockets close 4003, including DMs. Commit/signaling survive cancellation. Post-commit history/replay deny. Live observation fences new frames ([S4](realtime.md#real-time-sync-contract)). [Active DM peer and canonical retries](dms.md#new-send-and-canonical-retry-b3)

## Channel membership and Channel managers (#149, #231, #311)

- A usable grant captures the active Member's current revision. List usable grantees' id/name/Role/right without email. Unknown/foreign/inactive targets give 404 `member_not_found`. Current-grant add and visible absent removal are no-ops. Every Role with access may leave. Absent self-removal on an inaccessible Channel matches nonexistent 404. A retained stale self-grant can be removed.
- Owner/Admin with public access manages public grants. Usable private managers are current grantees with Owner/Admin Role or manager right. They add/remove grants and change rights. Private creation atomically grants the creator the right. Public Channels and Guests cannot hold rights.
- Right lasts one grant lifetime, independent of Role except Guest conversion clears it atomically. Promotion never restores it. Continuous access keeps grant id, Unread and subscriptions across Role/right changes. Right-only changes emit no loss. Leave/removal ends the lifetime. Renewal/re-grant starts without the right. A same-value PATCH is a no-op only after authorization.
- Manager PATCH errors are 403 `forbidden` for unauthorized actors (including Guests/no-ops), 404 `channel not found` for inaccessible/unknown/foreign Channel, 404 `member_not_found` for unknown/foreign/inactive target, 404 `channel_grant_not_found` for absent/stale target grant, 400 `channel_manager_private_only` after public management authorization and 400 `channel_manager_guest` even for no-op revoke.
- Revocation, removal/leave, Role change and deactivation deny 409 `last_channel_manager` if any managed private Channel would lose its last usable manager. Removal/leave checks 409 `last_channel_grant` first. Refusals give only the error value and change no Role, grant, Unread, Session or subscription.
- The メンバー tab shows the right and uses `can_manage_managers` for confirmed right changes. [Japanese refusals](../ui.md)

## Channel rename and archive (#410, proposed)

- L1. Only ordinary Channels (kind `channel`) are renamed, archived or restored. Public: active Owner/Admin. Private: a usable Channel manager (#311), i.e. a current grantee with Owner/Admin Role or the manager right. No Role bypasses private access. Other Members, Guests and non-manager grantees get 403 `forbidden`. Accessible DMs refuse all three with 400 `dm_channel_fixed` after DM access authorization (DMs have no name or archive state).
- L2. Archive is a Channel state, not access loss. Grants, manager rights, read positions, Unread baselines, Topics, Messages, subscriptions and #204 placements are kept, and restore brings all of them back. Rename/archive/restore allocate no `channel_log` position and change no Message version, `mutation_seq` floor, `history_generation`, fixed-W page or cursor (#160).
- L3. While archived, new sends (including Replies and first Topic Messages), Move and rename get 409 `channel_archived` after Channel authorization and before send limiting/admission (S6). History, events/replay/subscribe, Topic/Reply reads, mark-read, Search and grant/manager/leave stay available under existing access, including #311 guards. A canonical retry (S5) of a send committed before archive returns its original result. #287 decides Edit/Delete on archived Channels.
- L4. Rename/archive/restore lock the Channel row exclusively in the #149/#311 order (Organization → Channel → Members → grants). Sends and Moves hold their shared Channel lock through COMMIT and recheck archive state under it. Send-first commits the Message and its position before archive commits. Archive-first refuses the send with 409, with no position or publication. A token paid inside persistence is kept, as for a missing parent (S6). Tests cover both commit orders.
- L5. Rename follows creation name rules (400 before a write transaction) and the #51 uniqueness, which includes archived Channels: their names stay taken (409 `channel name already exists`). An exact-byte same name or same-state archive/restore is a no-op after authorization: 204, no write, no notice. A case-only rename is a change.
- L6. The ordinary Channel list excludes archived Channels. A separate archived list returns currently accessible archived Channels with the same access rules, order and Capabilities, and offers restore. While archived a Channel adds no Unread or Mention badge or total. Its read positions stay and count again after restore. Absence from the ordinary list is not access loss: only `channel_access_revoked`, `channel_access_lost` or a denial is (C9). A link to an archived Channel opens its history read-only.
- L7. Proposed API: `PATCH /api/v1/orgs/{org}/channels/{id}` with exactly one of `name` or `archived` (boolean), unknown fields rejected, empty 204. Archived list: `GET /api/v1/orgs/{org}/channels?archived=true` with the existing `ChannelList`. `Channel` gains `archived`. Capabilities gain `can_rename` and `can_archive` (archive and restore). Error order: 401 → 400 path/body/name → 404 `organization_unavailable` → 404 `channel not found` (inaccessible, unknown, foreign) → 400 `dm_channel_fixed` → 403 `forbidden` → 409. Denials write and publish nothing.
- L8. An append-only migration adds nullable `channels.archived_at` with a CHECK that DMs are never archived. No backfill: existing Channels stay active. `channels` owns the SQL. Any other schema change or backfill needs maintainer approval.
- L9. Web replaces the overview placeholder with 名前を変更, アーカイブ and 復元 controls gated by Capabilities, confirms archive and restore, adds the アーカイブ済みチャンネル list, and shows a read-only notice with sending disabled in archived Channels. Refusals are in Japanese. [Copy rules](../ui.md)

## Setup

- Bootstrap creates only the first Organization and Owner. Repeat gives 409 `setup already completed` after token/input validation. Public read-only GET exposes only no-store `setup_required` independent of the token.
- `FUKULOW_SETUP_TOKEN` is required in all modes. Missing, blank or former published example (including surrounding whitespace) fails startup. Keep exact bytes and never generate/log it. [Deployment](deployment.md#proxy-and-sign-in-limits)
- POST extraction comes first (malformed/missing/invalid JSON 400, oversized 413, unsupported media 415). Then missing/wrong token gives the same generic 401 before semantic validation, hashing or database access, before and after setup.
