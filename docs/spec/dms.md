## Model and permissions

- D1. A Direct message (DM) is a Channel of kind `dm` for exactly two distinct Members of one Organization. “Participant” identifies either retained Member regardless of current access. DMs have no name, visibility, Topics, Move to topic, participant management or leave. Replies and Reply chains are allowed with `topic_id:null`. Ordinary kind `channel` retains its visibility and manager rules.
- D2. Order the two Member ids by ascending UUID value and enforce unique `(organization_id, lower_member_id, higher_member_id)` in storage. Use Member identities. Keep the pair, Channel id and R4 creation key immutable through deactivation, reactivation, Role/name changes and renewal. Same-Organization composite references, ordered/distinct pair checks and grant constraints reject foreign or third participants even in direct SQL. New tables require `organization_id NOT NULL` and Organization context/RLS. Channels owns pair/grant SQL. Messages owns seq, Messages and read positions. Auth supplies scoped identity and eligibility.
- D3. Every Role needs active Organization Membership, matching captured revision, pair participation and a usable grant for that revision. Each participant has one explicit grant with a durable `grant_id` and a captured `membership_revision`. No public or administrative bypass, including with a third-party grant. Share the deny-by-default `authorize` policy and Channels visibility predicate across lists, history, Reply chains, send/retry, mark-read, subscribe, replay, live, #152 Mentions and #153 Search. SQL visibility and position triggers must agree.
- D4. Only an active Owner/Admin/Member of that Organization can open or renew, including existing DMs. Create/renew permits any active same-Organization peer Role, including Guest. Resolving a usable pair skips peer eligibility (B2.1). Guests cannot open but current Guest grantees may list, select, read, send, Reply, mark-read and subscribe. Selection uses reads/subscription. Knowing a Member id grants only the opportunity for a separately authorized open.
- D5. DM grants have no manager right. All four Capabilities (`can_add_members`, `can_remove_members`, `can_manage_managers`, `can_leave`) deny with `dm_membership_fixed`. Every Channel member/grant API refuses after DM access authorization, before target/management validation, including GET, self-leave, stale cleanup and no-ops. Only `POST /dms` renews grants. Exclude DMs from `RoleChangeGuard::lock`, last-grant guards and all #311/#292 usable-manager counts, SQL projections and Capabilities calculations. DM demotion/deactivation never causes `last_channel_manager`. Ordinary safeguards and `last_owner` precedence hold. Role changes alone preserve usable DM grants, even on Guest demotion.
- D6. Append-only migration preserves all existing Channel ids, names, visibility, grant lifetimes/rights, seq, Messages and Unread, backfilling kind `channel`. Preserve Organization-wide ordinary name validation and uniqueness across visibility under `lower(name COLLATE "C")` (ASCII-only fold). DMs occupy no name slot. No kind or API path may bypass ordinary name uniqueness or assign DMs ordinary management or visibility.

## Open and renewal (B2)

- B2.1. After caller authorization, omitted/false `renew` resolves an existing pair with a usable caller grant as 200 before peer eligibility, even when the peer is inactive or lapsed. No grant, position or publication changes. Otherwise create the pair, Channel, both grants and baselines atomically (201), or renew every lapsed grant of either participant in the existing pair (200). Create/renew requires an active same-Organization peer. Use `renew:true` to renew a lapsed peer when the caller has access. Fresh active non-Guest caller authority plus active peer permits this renewal even without a current caller grant. No other operation gains authority. Never renew for an inactive peer or third Member, replace a current grant, or borrow a reactivated caller's new revision.
- B2.2. Renewed grants capture current revision with new `grant_id` and no manager right. Current grants retain id, Unread and subscription. New/renewed grants initialize Unread at the latest committed seq (0 when empty). Hold #142 access serialization and shared #135 through access COMMIT so no send commits between baseline and visibility. Earlier history stays readable but contributes neither Unread nor Mention unread. A duplicate open with both grants current is a reauthorized no-op.
- B2.3. Lock Organization NO KEY UPDATE → existing DM Channel → both Members by ascending id → grants by ascending Member id → discovery counters/index (P-D2) → #142/shared #135 for grant changes. For creation, serialize absence under the Organization lock before Members/new rows, with storage uniqueness as final defense. Recheck caller Role/activity/captured revision and pair/caller grant under their locks through COMMIT, including resolution-only no-ops. Only create/renew checks peer activity/revision/eligibility there. Changed preliminary lookup restarts before later locks. Never take an earlier lock afterwards.
- B2.4. Reciprocal concurrent opens produce the same id with one 201 and one 200. Concurrent open/renewal replaces each lapsed lifetime once without resetting Unread twice or making a duplicate/partial pair. Open-first may commit before deactivation stales the affected grant. Deactivation-first denies the caller with `organization_unavailable` or denies create/renew for peer loss with `member_not_found`. A still-authorized resolution-only caller instead gets 200 with `peer.active:false`. Concurrent reactivation must pass fresh locked authorization before renewal. Refusal/rollback changes neither participant's grant or position.
- B2.5. Recover an unknown open by finding `peer.id` in the authorized R4 list. If the DM id is unknown, follow `next_cursor` until found or exhausted. One page's omission proves nothing. Use a separate recovery continuation independent of browsing and retention (E6). Match accepted rows before eviction. Invalidations preserve progress and stale pages retry the same boundary. Same-`member_id` resolution-only retry returns 200 for the committed id with current authorized fields while caller access remains usable, even after peer deactivation/lapse. Inactive/stale caller gets 404 `organization_unavailable`. Guest gets 403 `forbidden` even with access. An active non-Guest without a usable grant gets no pair information before peer eligibility. Unknown/inactive/foreign peers give uniform 404 `member_not_found`. For an active same-Organization peer B2.1 create/renew applies. Explicit renewal retry rechecks the peer and may fail after a prior COMMIT. Refetch can still recover a caller-accessible DM. Pair uniqueness requires no client create id and does not guarantee retry success after authority changes. Definitive refusals are never automatically retried. Terminal C6/C10 stops recovery until explicit reauthorization.

## REST contract

Routes are under `/api/v1/orgs/{org}` with existing cookie/bearer authority and `Cache-Control: no-store` on every response, including errors. Open rejects unknown fields. Organization/caller/revision/grant are resolved by the server. Open uses the 16 KiB body limit. Send uses 32 KiB. [REST and event shapes](../../openapi.json)

`id` is the underlying Channel id in REST, events and URLs. `peer` provides the other participant's current snapshot name/id, retained while inactive, without email, Account id, grant or revision. `peer.active` reports Membership activity only. It neither grants directory access nor promises send eligibility. Discriminate by kind. DMs omit name/visibility. The separate `GET /dms` list includes accessible DMs. `/channels` and personal Channel sections exclude DMs for every Role.

Reused Message history and Reply-chain routes keep ordinary paging and do not check peer eligibility. The main timeline has only Message lines and retains P2–P3 watermarks. Send/Reply uses the common canonical Message and 201 including retry (B3). A Reply needs a committed parent in this DM and inherits null Topic. Mark-read covers all DM Messages, including Replies, with monotonic persisted `read_seq` clamped to the committed seq and grant baseline. History/Reply reads alone never mark-read. After Channel authorization, the DM Topic refusal applies even if the requested Topic does not exist.

- R1. A read-only REPEATABLE READ snapshot shares authorization, peer identity, committed `as_of_seq`, positions and counts without Member row locks. Batch owning-module queries with no per-DM Member/Unread SQL. Limit to 50 authorized ids before aggregating, with one extra authorized id permitted for cursor lookahead. Empty authorized lists succeed. Inaccessible rows are silently omitted. A caller-accessible DM remains listed even if the peer is inactive or their grant has lapsed. Unavailable/stale callers get `organization_unavailable`. POST returns the same fields from its committed transaction, without promising a response-time watermark or replacing newer accepted baselines.
- R2. DM Unread follows P8 with no Topic scopes. `main_unread_count = unread_count` counts other senders' Messages above `read_seq` through `as_of_seq`. Sending moves no position. `mention_unread_count` counts the #152 matching subset once per Message in that same snapshot, visibility, sender exclusion, grant baseline and read scope. It is zero before accepted Mentions exist, never a placeholder once persisted, and bounded by Unread. It reveals no inaccessible Message and adds no read cursor. #152 owns token/target semantics.
- R3 (proposed Search). #153 uses D3 before exposing hits, snippets, authors, facets or counts. Owner/Admin or Mention targeting adds no access. Peer deactivation keeps results available to the current participant, while stale caller access denies. Search never opens/renews. #152 resolves identities/counts only within authorized resources and cannot widen the Guest directory. Share D3 authority, permission fixtures and generated API types across #151/#152/#153.
- R4. Browse 50 at a time in immutable `(created_at DESC, id DESC)` order (UUID-value tie break), starting without a cursor and continuing strictly below the last key. `next_cursor` is null exactly when no further authorized row exists in that snapshot. Opaque integrity-protected cursors bind Organization, Member, captured revision, Account Session and order version. Reauthorize each request. A lost anchor grants no access and does not prevent browsing below it. A stable set traverses once without gaps/duplicates, even at equal timestamps. Additions or access changes require a fresh first page/new traversal. Rename, activity, send and renewal never move a creation key. There is no shared cross-page MVCC snapshot.

  Held-id refresh accepts 1..=50 distinct `channel_id` values, exclusive with `cursor`, and returns only accessible requested rows in the same order with `next_cursor:null`. Nonexistent, foreign, nonparticipant and lapsed-grant ids are indistinguishably omitted, without total/hidden metadata. No client `limit`. Current caller authority precedes malformed/tampered/wrong-scope cursor or invalid query errors (400 `invalid input`). The Organization history epoch follows C8/#160 for open/list responses and generation-aware list queries, including empty lists. Stale supplied list generation gives 409 `history_generation_changed` before cursor validation and data. Cursor state confers no authority or server snapshot.

  Deduplicate by Channel id and merge R1/R2 tuples within one access generation (E6/P8). Never sum duplicates, mix response positions/counts or treat unloaded pages as zero. No Organization-wide badge total. Explicit browsing/recovery can traverse pages. Periodic reconciliation does not walk browse cursors (E5).

### New send and canonical retry (B3)

- B3.1. Authenticate Session → validate input → authorize caller Organization/Channel → check canonical `(organization_id,sender,client_message_id)`. Do the lookup before peer eligibility, limiter or admission. Same committed intent returns 201 with the original Message identity and current canonical state (S5) even after peer loss/lapse. Changed body/Channel/Topic intent/parent conflicts. Deactivated, stale, lapsed or nonparticipant senders get their ordinary denial even on retry, without the canonical body.
- B3.2. Only new sends need an active peer with a current-revision grant through COMMIT. Inactive peer gives 409 `dm_peer_deactivated`. Active lapsed peer gives 409 `dm_peer_access_lapsed` and needs explicit B2 renewal. Resolution-only open and sending never renew it. Neither failure consumes seq, writes a Message or position, or publishes.
- B3.3. After a canonical miss reject non-null DM Topic intent before limiter/admission. An already committed changed-intent retry conflicts first. Limiter precedes admission under S6. The definitive peer check is transactional, so 429/503 may precede peer denial. Reauthorize captured sender revision/grant and recheck canonical intent before peer eligibility, then Reply parent, then allocation/insert. Apply S6's no-refund and differing-intent rules. Identical in-flight duplicates wait and return the committed winner after fresh sender authorization without applying peer checks. A unique-key loser rolls back allocation and reauthorizes before reading that winner without a new-send peer check.
- B3.4. New sends lock both Members FOR SHARE in ascending id before grants in the same order, retaining sender captured and peer current authority through COMMIT. A sender-only grant lock followed by a peer Member lock is forbidden. Canonical fast lookup needs no peer lock. A transactional duplicate does not persist or require peer eligibility. Follow Organization → Channel → Members → grants, skipping unneeded mutation locks without acquiring them later. Take P-D2 counters/index after Member/grant locks, before compatible FK locks and exclusive #135/Message counter. Keep module boundaries and non-locking post-COMMIT recipient queries.
- B3.5. Send-first holds peer Member/grant through COMMIT, then deactivation stales the grant. Deactivation-first denies under lock. Renewal/send uses a still-current or newly committed lifetime, never mixed/stale. A lapsed peer denies. Test both sender directions and reciprocal lock order. Shared read/mark-read authority excludes peer eligibility, keeping the current participant's operations available.

### Refusals and precedence

POST `/dms` authenticates, parses the Organization, resolves current Membership, validates input, checks non-Guest, rejects self, then inspects the same-Organization pair and caller grant under B2 locks. Reauthorize after lock waits, including no-ops; no stale caller may use the resolution branch. Only a usable caller grant allows resolution before peer eligibility (B2). GET `/dms` authorizes the caller before cursor validation and filters by D3. Reused routes keep S/P extraction and validation precedence and expose DM-specific errors only after Channel authorization. A valid Reply with Topic intent keeps the validation-first 400 `reply_topic_not_allowed`. Malformed Topic names fail ordinary validation. Denied/rolled-back mutations write and publish nothing. Uncertain COMMIT follows A5–A6/O5.

|---|---|
| Missing, invalid, expired or ended Account Session | 401 `invalid credentials` |
| Unknown Organization, no Membership, deactivated caller, captured revision stale (including after reactivation) | 404 `organization_unavailable` |
| Malformed UUID/JSON/query, unknown/missing fields, wrong types, invalid seq/limit/body or Topic syntax | 400 `invalid input` |
| Body exceeds route limit / unsupported body content type | 413 `request body too large` / 415 `expected application/json` |
| Guest calls POST `/dms`, including an existing pair or no-op | 403 `forbidden` |
| Open with oneself | 400 `dm_self_not_allowed` |
| Open on the create/renew branch (including `renew:true`) with unknown, inactive or other-Organization peer | 404 `member_not_found` (same body whether a pair exists or not; no id/grant/position disclosed). An accessible pair with `renew` omitted/false instead returns 200 before this check, even for an inactive peer. |
| Unknown/foreign DM id, non-participant of any Role, or caller's missing/lapsed participant grant, on any reused per-Channel route including canonical retry | 404 `channel not found`, identical to an inaccessible private Channel |
| Cursor page or held-id list request by a valid Member without access to a particular DM | 200 list omitting it; no placeholder, per-id error or hidden-pair count; cursor grants no access |

| Unknown, foreign or uncommitted Reply parent / unknown or foreign Reply-chain anchor, after Channel authorization | 404 `reply_parent_not_found` / 404 `reply_chain_not_found` |
| Reply with non-null Topic intent (existing validation) | 400 `reply_topic_not_allowed` |
| Non-Reply Topic send or any Topic route in an accessible DM, including future routes such as Move to topic | 400 `dm_topics_not_allowed` |
| Any Channel member/grant API on an accessible DM, including Guest and no-op/self operations | 400 `dm_membership_fixed` |
| Send bucket exhausted | 429 `send_rate_limited`, existing Retry-After semantics |
| Send admission / send-limiter tracking / projection or watermark admission unavailable | 503 `send admission unavailable` / `send limiter unavailable` / `projection admission unavailable`, existing Retry-After semantics |

WebSocket subscribe for an inaccessible DM uses the existing asynchronous `channel_not_found` error (no HTTP ErrorBody or existence distinction); a duplicate subscription remains S4's charged no-op. No replay/live/notice payload reaches an unauthorized Member. Channel grant loss uses `channel_access_lost` for an existing subscription; Organization Membership loss uses 4003 as below. No DM refusal reports the peer/pair/Channel details beyond its error value.

## Deactivation and reactivation

- L1. Deactivation increments revision and invalidates the participant's DM grants/positions. Close idle/replaying sockets across their Account Sessions for that Membership with terminal 4003/C10, never `channel_access_lost`. Other Organizations and the active participant's sessions stay unaffected. S4 fallback closes old revisions within 30 s + 5 s if the signal is lost. Even before fallback and after reactivation, old revisions cannot subscribe, replay, receive Messages or receive notices. Observed loss fences queued/fetched frames and first handoff. Only already handed-off frames may finish.
- L2. The current participant retains grant, positions, list/history/Reply/Search, mark-read, subscribe/replay and B2 resolution-only open. B2 create/renew and new sends/Replies require peer eligibility. List keeps the inactive peer with `active:false`. Refusal publishes no Message/discovery activity. Reactivation alone restores no access, positions, access-gained event, subscription or grant id. A reactivated participant's new current-revision login/reselection sees no DM access until explicit renewal.
- L3. Renewal keeps Channel/history and replaces only lapsed grants. Old registrations/subscriptions, buffered notices, delayed publisher queries, HTTP responses and marks remain fenced even for the same Member id. Subscribe/reselect afresh. Only an eligible non-Guest participant can renew. The peer may be Guest. If both are Guests with lapsed grants, a permitted Role change must precede open. No automatic restoration or third-party Admin open.

## DM discovery and activity notices (B4)

- E1. `dm_list_changed` is an A1–A14 ServerEvent with only its snake_case `type`. It invalidates one Member's list structure/peer identity, addressed by Organization, Member and revision. Register once per socket under A1 and existing caps, including zero subscriptions. No Message-registry, Organization broadcast or client subscribe variant. Every recipient needs a live Session and active current Membership. Ordinary notices also require a usable participant grant to the causing DM. Even empty payloads and failure/reset timing must not disclose a hidden DM.
- E2. Reuse `channel_activity` (authorized other-sender peer sessions) and `read_position_changed` (marking Member's authorized sessions). They carry the ordinary Channel id, seq/position and recipient semantics. Route structural/identity changes without a Message or seq to `dm_list_changed` and the independent `/dms` target, never `channel_list_changed`. Common Message/Reply events use null Topic and no DM variant. Authorized lists supply kind. #152 adds counts without a new notice. #153 authorizes by D3/R3.

| Committed mutation | Notices and affected Members |
|---|---|
| New pair, including empty DM | `dm_list_changed` to each participant's own eligible sessions; `channel_access_gained {channel_id}` to each newly granted participant (access-generation fence). |
| Explicit renewal | `dm_list_changed` to both participants with current access after COMMIT; `channel_access_gained` only to each renewed participant, not the participant whose grant stayed current. |
| Resolution-only open with usable caller grant, or eligible renewal with both grants current | None (reauthorized no-op; resolution never renews a peer grant). |
| New Message or Reply | `channel_activity {channel_id,seq}` to the authorized peer Member's sessions only, even when they view another conversation or have never selected this DM. No own-send notice; canonical retries emit none. |
| Mark-read raises position | `read_position_changed {channel_id,topic_id:null,read_seq}` only to that Member's own authorized sessions, including the caller. No-op max emits none. |
| Participant deactivation | `dm_list_changed` to the other participant if they still have access (peer display/eligibility changed); target sockets close 4003. Do not send the peer access-revoked or the target a grant-loss event. |

## B4 durable discovery (Option A adopted, 2026-11-14)

Process every still-accessible DM automatically, including empty/old DMs, gains and new Messages, despite lost notices or delayed COMMIT and without browsing or a later mutation. Processing means accepting an authorized identity before eviction, without promising retained rows or baselines. Access must remain usable through a generation-current authorized response, with no disclosure of lost transient access. Outages, backoff and invalidated responses are no progress.

Option A uses a durable Member/revision index coalesced by DM. Mutable last-activity lists/sweeps (Option B) cannot guarantee this. Independent rolling delta and frozen birth coverage each get a reserved O5 slot. No event archive, per-client server snapshot, extra poller or unbounded client state.

### Protocol and amendment of E4-E6

- P-D1 (durability/ownership). Channels owns discovery SQL, index and counter. Messages calls a narrow transaction operation. Auth supplies scoped authority. - P-D2 (commit order). Allocate with a transactional counter-row update held through COMMIT per Organization/Member/revision, never a sequence, clock or max(id). Lock Organization → existing Channel → both Members by ascending id → existing grants by ascending Member id → both counters by ascending Member id → index rows → #142/shared or exclusive #135 → Message seq. Create locks counters before new grants/triggered baselines, renewal locks counters/index after existing grants but before changing them, and send locks counters/index before exclusive #135/seq.   Counters/index, grants/baselines and Message persistence commit or roll back together. Rollback may reuse its invisible position.   **Coverage.** Start K=0 and freeze birth ceiling B at the committed counter. Scan K < birth_position ≤ B ascending, regardless of latest_position. Advance only K. Bumps cannot hide a pending birth.
